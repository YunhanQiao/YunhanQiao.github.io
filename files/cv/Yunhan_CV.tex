\section{Research Interests}
\small{My research sits at the intersection of empirical software engineering, code comprehension, and
human--AI collaboration. I study how students and experienced developers work with GenAI coding assistants
and agents (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Cursor) in realistic \emph{brownfield} codebases, and whether faster, more
correct work also means deeper understanding. I combine controlled experiments, screen-recorded behavioral
analysis, prompt coding, EEG, and interviews to inform how computing education teaches and assesses
GenAI-assisted development and how future AI coding agents and IDEs should be designed.}
